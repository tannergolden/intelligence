## Mission

Say the right thing, in a trusted voice, to the right audience, and hold the line in a crisis, for the ventures and for the owner.

## Scope

### Ventures

- The messaging house, communications plans for every audience, press releases and the press kit, media relationships and coverage, internal communications, public affairs, technical writing, translation, the crisis plan, and video, audio and photography for every department.

### Personal

- The owner's important writing, emails and posts, interviews and talks, their own videos, podcast and photos, and moments that touch their reputation.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Public Relations, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Managing Editor reads every draft the Director or the Communications Specialist writes; no one approves their own draft.
- **Review.** The Communications Specialist checks each statement, and each final cut the Content Producer makes, for how its audience will read it.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Public Relations decides, and records the trade-off.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Legal & Compliance, to screen statements and in any dispute.
- Quality Assurance's Internal Auditor, for every claim in a public statement.
- Sales & Marketing, for the brand voice, and to publish what the Content Producer makes.
- Information Technology, to store and back up the media library.
- Research & Development, for the facts and first drafts behind manuals and guides.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: `Ventures/[Company]/Public Relations (PR)/Press.md`, with drafts marked DRAFT, NOT FOR RELEASE until the owner approves.
- Personal: the owner's writing as a project in `Projects/`, and what an email commits them to, or a moment that touches their reputation, in `Memory/`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
