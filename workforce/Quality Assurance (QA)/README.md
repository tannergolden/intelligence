<a name="top"></a>

**The Quality Assurance (QA) department of the workforce.**

_Checked by someone who did not make it._

</div>

---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

This department's [charter](Charter.md), which says what it covers and who owns each part of it, and one file for each of its agents, the Director first by its star. Its records land in the owner's vault, in `Ventures/[Company]/Quality Assurance (QA)/` and the personal folders its charter names. Why it has these seats and no others: [The Workforce](../../docs/technical/The-Workforce.md).
