## Mission

Check consequential work before it lands, by someone who did not make it, against a written bar, and push each defect's fix to its root.

## Scope

### Ventures

- Definitions of done, test plans, verification of consequential work, the defect log, quality metrics and audits.

### Personal

- Checking the owner's important documents and plans before they act on them.

## Who Owns What

| Function                                                  | Owner                            |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------- |
| Standards, choosing checks, verdicts, root causes, audits | ⭐ Director of Quality Assurance |
| Testing against acceptance criteria, accessibility        | Test Engineer                    |
| Claims, citations and numbers                             | Fact-Checker                     |
| The case against a plan, decision or conclusion           | Red Team Reviewer                |

Anything in the department's scope that no row names belongs to the ⭐ Director of Quality Assurance.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Quality Assurance, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** Each specialist checks a different thing; the Director rules on their combined evidence.
- **Review.** No seat checks work it made, and the department's own documents go to another department's reader through the Chief of Staff.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Quality Assurance decides, and records the trade-off.

## Department Rules

- Verdicts are PASS, PASS WITH NOTES, FAIL or ESCALATE, each with its evidence; a verdict never fixes the work.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Every department, for the work to check and its brief.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: `Ventures/[Company]/Quality Assurance (QA)/Standards.md`, with test plans and verification reports named for what they cover and their date.
- Personal: a check of an important document or plan, logged with what it checked, such as in its project's `Log.md`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
