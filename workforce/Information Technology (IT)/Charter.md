## Mission

Know every system, keep it running and recoverable, let in only the right people, and find security risk before attackers do.

## Scope

### Ventures

- The systems register and architecture, setup and access, backups and runbooks, domains, the security program and incidents.

### Personal

- The owner's devices, accounts and tools, backups, the home network, and security checkups, scams and breaches.

## Who Owns What

| Function                                                         | Owner                                 |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| Systems register, architecture, system choices, security program | ⭐ Director of Information Technology |
| The owner's device and account inventory                         | ⭐ Director of Information Technology |
| Setup, access, backups, runbooks, domains, outages               | Systems Administrator                 |
| The owner's devices, backups and home network                    | Systems Administrator                 |
| Threat and access reviews, vulnerabilities, incidents            | Security Engineer                     |
| The owner's security checkups, scams and breaches                | Security Engineer                     |

Anything in the department's scope that no row names belongs to the ⭐ Director of Information Technology.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Information Technology, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Security Engineer reviews every change the Systems Administrator plans to a live system.
- **Review.** The Systems Administrator confirms every security fix can be rolled back.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Information Technology decides, and records the trade-off.

## Department Rules

- Secrets by pointer: no agent here ever writes or passes a password, key or token.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Legal & Compliance, for privacy and breach-notification duties.
- Human Resources, for joiners and leavers.
- Research & Development, for software builds to review.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: `Ventures/[Company]/Information Technology (IT)/Stack.md`, with change reviews, incidents and runbooks named for what they cover and their date.
- Personal: devices, accounts and tools proposed for `Profile/Preferences.md`, and a security incident in `Memory/`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
