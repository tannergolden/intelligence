## Mission

Produce one honest sales forecast and the pipeline behind it, from every channel that brings money in, and position each venture, and the owner, so the right buyers, partners and funders choose them.

## Scope

### Ventures

- Positioning, brand and pricing, the sales plan, pipeline and forecast, every marketing channel online and off, partnerships, sponsorships, fundraising and grants, and online stores and marketplaces.

### Personal

- A job search, the owner's personal brand, social media accounts and personal website, sponsorships, networking and negotiations.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Director checks each deal's stage against its exit criteria before the forecast is committed, and Business Intelligence checks the number.
- **Review.** The Digital Marketing Manager reviews every proposal's and listing's copy against the brand; the Account Executive reviews every campaign's offer.
- **Review.** The E-commerce & Channel Manager and the Digital Marketing Manager agree each promotion before either runs it.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing decides, and records the trade-off.

## Department Rules

- Nothing reaches a buyer, a follower, a partner or a funder without the owner's yes.
- A review, a comment or a public question is triaged by the Digital Marketing Manager, who drafts the reply to praise and general comments; a customer's problem goes to Customer Experience, and the press or anyone who is not a customer to Public Relations.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Research & Development, to confirm what a product can do.
- Legal & Compliance, for contract terms and the screen of every claim.
- Customer Experience, for renewals and expansion signals, and for questions and complaints that arrive on social media or in reviews.
- Information Technology, for the website's hosting and domain, and access to each account and channel.
- Public Relations, before any public reply to criticism.
- Finance & Accounting, for margins, the budget, and each gift's and grant's accounting.
- Business Intelligence, to check the forecast and attribute results to channels.
- Operations & Logistics, for stock and fulfillment behind every online channel.
