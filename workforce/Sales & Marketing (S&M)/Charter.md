## Mission

Produce one honest revenue number and the pipeline behind it, and position each venture, and the owner, so the right buyers choose them.

## Scope

### Ventures

- Positioning and brand, products, services and pricing, the sales plan, pipeline and forecast, and the revenue and demand plans.

### Personal

- A job search, the owner's personal brand, networking and negotiations.

## Who Owns What

| Function                                                      | Owner                            |
| :------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------- |
| Revenue number, forecast commit, pricing policy, go-to-market | ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing |
| Job search strategy and negotiation walk-away                 | ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing |
| Deals, proposals, quotes, negotiations                        | Account Executive                |
| Brand, positioning, campaigns, content, launches, demand plan | Marketing Manager                |
| The owner's personal brand and content                        | Marketing Manager                |
| Stages, system of record, forecast math, funnel, quota        | Revenue Operations Analyst       |
| Job-search tracker and networking list                        | Revenue Operations Analyst       |

Anything in the department's scope that no row names belongs to the ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Revenue Operations Analyst checks each deal's stage against its exit criteria before the forecast is committed.
- **Review.** The Marketing Manager reviews every proposal's positioning; the Account Executive reviews every campaign's offer.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Sales & Marketing decides, and records the trade-off.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Research & Development, to confirm what a product can do.
- Legal & Compliance, for contract terms and claims screening.
- Customer Experience, for renewals and expansion signals.
- Finance & Accounting, for pricing margins and the revenue plan.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: `Ventures/[Company]/Sales & Marketing (S&M)/Brand.md`, `Products.md`, `Services.md`, `Sales-Plan.md`, `Pipeline.md` and `Revenue.md`.
- Personal: a job search or personal brand as a project in `Projects/`, people met in `Memory/`, and what a negotiation settles in `Memory/Decisions/`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
