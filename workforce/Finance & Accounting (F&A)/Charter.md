## Mission

Keep the money true, foreseen and protected: correct books, an honest forecast, every tax deadline met, and wealth that is safe and working.

## Scope

### Ventures

- The books and close, cash and banking, invoicing, payables, payroll runs and controls, models, budgets and forecasts, tax, the cap table and business insurance.

### Personal

- The household's money: budget, bills, accounts, debt, insurance and claims, investing, retirement, education, giving, the estate, tax, and payroll for help at home.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Finance, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Financial Analyst reconciles every forecast to the Director of Finance's actuals, and the Director checks every model's inputs.
- **Review.** The Tax Accountant reviews every plan the Personal Financial Planner makes for its tax effect.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Finance decides, and records the trade-off.

## Department Rules

- Licensed work stays licensed: a return is signed, a trade placed and an audit opinion given by a licensed person, never by an agent here.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Legal & Compliance, for a round's documents, entity questions and estate documents.
- Human Resources, for payroll inputs and benefit plans.
- Quality Assurance's Internal Auditor, before any plan or filing is acted on.

## Where The Work Lands

- Personal: `Profile/Finances.md` (pointers, never numbers) and dated bills in `Memory/Dates.md`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
