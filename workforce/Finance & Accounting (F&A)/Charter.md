## Mission

Keep the money true, foreseen and protected: correct books, an honest forecast, every tax deadline met, and wealth that is safe and working.

## Scope

### Ventures

- The books and monthly close, the financial model and budget, the cap table, company tax, treasury and benefit plans.

### Personal

- Bills, spending, budget, debt, savings goals, taxes, investing, retirement, insurance and estate essentials.

## Who Owns What

| Function                                                   | Owner                          |
| :--------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| Capital allocation, runway, budget approval, the cap table | ⭐ Director of Finance         |
| The owner's whole financial picture                        | ⭐ Director of Finance         |
| Monthly close, reconciliations, payables and receivables   | Controller                     |
| The owner's bills and spending against budget              | Controller                     |
| Financial model, budget, forecasts, scenarios, variance    | Financial Analyst              |
| The owner's budget plan, debt payoff, affordability        | Financial Analyst              |
| Company and personal tax, equity tax events                | Tax Strategist                 |
| Treasury, benefit plans, founder liquidity                 | Investment & Insurance Advisor |
| The owner's investing, retirement, insurance, estate       | Investment & Insurance Advisor |

Anything in the department's scope that no row names belongs to the ⭐ Director of Finance.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Finance, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Financial Analyst builds on the Controller's closed actuals, and flags any actual that looks wrong.
- **Review.** The Tax Strategist reviews the tax effect of every plan the Financial Analyst or the Investment & Insurance Advisor makes.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Finance decides, and records the trade-off.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Legal & Compliance, for a round's documents and entity questions.
- Human Resources, for payroll and benefit plans.
- Quality Assurance's Fact-Checker, before any plan or filing is acted on.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: `Ventures/[Company]/Finance & Accounting (F&A)/Financials.md`, `Financial-Model.md` and `Cap-Table.md`.
- Personal: `Profile/Finances.md` (pointers, never numbers) and dated bills in `Memory/Dates.md`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
