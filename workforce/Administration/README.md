<a name="top"></a>

**The Administration department of the workforce.**

_Direction set once, decided in the open, written down._

</div>

---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

This department's [charter](Charter.md), which says what it covers and who owns each part of it, and one file for each of its agents: the ⭐ Chief Executive Officer, its Director, first by its star, then the Chiefs, each overseeing the departments whose Directors report to it. Its records land in the owner's vault, in `Ventures/[Company]/Administration/` and the personal folders its charter names. Why it has these seats and no others: [The Workforce](../../docs/technical/The-Workforce.md).
