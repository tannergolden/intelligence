## Mission

Run the workforce as a company's executive team runs a company: set the direction, decide what crosses departments, run the operating rhythm, and oversee every department through its Director.

## Scope

### Ventures

- Strategy, decisions across portfolios, each venture's stage, founding and corporate development, the board and the decision record, OKRs and the operating cadence, capital, funding and risk, technology direction, and revenue and pricing.

### Personal

- Life strategy and big decisions, goals, the quarter and the week, money between the owner and the ventures, technology choices, and income strategy.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Chief Executive Officer, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Chief Financial Officer checks the numbers in every plan and pre-read before it leaves the executive team.
- **Review.** The ⭐ Chief Executive Officer hears every Chief whose portfolio a decision touches before deciding it.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Chief Executive Officer decides, and records the trade-off.

## Department Rules

- Each department's Director reports to the Chief who oversees it, named in the directory, and that Chief reaches it only through the Chief of Staff; the Chief of Staff delivers each portfolio's escalations straight to its Chief.
- A Chief decides within its portfolio and the owner's mandate, and never a question a Director can decide.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Every department, for its view and its numbers on a decision.
- Business Intelligence, for the governed numbers behind the scorecard.
- Quality Assurance's Red Team Reviewer, before a consequential decision.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: its own `Overview.md`, `Goals.md` and `Log.md`, and `Administration/Strategy.md`, `Decisions.md` and `Cadence.md`, with board and reporting packages named for what they cover and their date.
- Personal: `Memory/Decisions/`, `Memory/Dates.md`, and proposals for `Profile/Goals/` and `Profile/Focus.md`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
