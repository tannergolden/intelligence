## Mission

Set the direction the other departments pull toward, make or frame the decisions that span them, and keep the plans and the record that show whether it is working.

## Scope

### Ventures

- Strategy, decisions across departments, the venture's stage, founding, OKRs, the operating cadence, board and governance records.

### Personal

- Life strategy, goals and priorities, the weekly plan, and the record of the owner's big decisions.

## Who Owns What

| Function                                         | Owner                         |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------- |
| Strategy, the venture's stage, founding          | ⭐ Director of Administration |
| Decisions and conflicts across departments       | ⭐ Director of Administration |
| OKRs, review loops, scorecard, execution plans   | Strategy & Planning Manager   |
| The owner's goals, quarter and week              | Strategy & Planning Manager   |
| Decision memos and log, board materials, minutes | Corporate Secretary           |
| The owner's personal decision record             | Corporate Secretary           |

Anything in the department's scope that no row names belongs to the ⭐ Director of Administration.

## Inside The Department

- **Intake.** The Chief of Staff delivers requests from other departments to the ⭐ Director of Administration, who assigns each to one seat.
- **Mesh.** Any seat here may message any other directly to collaborate, review or hand work over.
- **Review.** The Strategy & Planning Manager drafts plans; the Director approves them.
- **Review.** The Corporate Secretary records each decision; the Director confirms the entry says what was decided.
- **Disagreement.** The ⭐ Director of Administration decides, and records the trade-off.

## What This Department Asks Others For

Always through the Chief of Staff, never directly:

- Every department, for its view on a decision, through the Chief of Staff.
- Business Intelligence, for the numbers behind the scorecard.
- Quality Assurance's Red Team Reviewer, before a consequential decision.

## Where The Work Lands

- Venture: `Ventures/[Company]/Overview.md`, `Goals.md`, `Log.md`, and `Administration/Strategy.md`, `Decisions.md` and `Cadence.md`.
- Personal: `Memory/Decisions/`, `Memory/Dates.md`, and proposals for `Profile/Goals/` and `Profile/Focus.md`.

The Knowledge Vault Manager files every record, by the vault's `AGENTS.md` and the README of the folder it lands in.
