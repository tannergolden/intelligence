# Generative brief (the Creative Lead fills this in Phase 0)

A self-contained brief that frames the challenge before any generation, threaded into every Idea Generator dispatch so each parallel worker generates against the real problem rather than improvising it.

- **The challenge:** [the raw problem or question, as the Founder gave it]
- **How might we:** [one or more HMW questions at the right altitude: broad enough for many solutions, narrow enough for traction, with no baked-in solution]
- **Desired outcome:** [what a great answer would achieve; the so-what]
- **Constraints and context:** [hard limits, the audience, what is fixed versus open, any evidence or data that grounds it]
- **Assigned lenses:** [one distinct lens per Idea Generator, from references/lenses.md, chosen to spread the field]
- **Tier:** [quick | standard | deep/portfolio]
- **Serves:** [the venture, project, or topic this is for, or "general"]
- **Deliver:** [a ranked shortlist in chat by default; file idea records to Inbox/Ideas/ and/or a discovery report to Inbox/Reports/ only on request]
