## How This File Works

- **Tracks** changes to how the skill works: tiers, the team, references, templates, the output contract. Not below-PATCH tweaks.
- **Bump** PATCH for a fix, MINOR for a backward-compatible addition, MAJOR for a breaking change. Update the Current Version line and `metadata.version` with every entry.
- **4-entry cap** per section; a fifth drops the oldest, which the repository's history keeps.

## Major Updates

> Breaking changes, restructured pipeline, incompatible output. Most recent 4 entries.

### `2.0.0` - 2026-11-07

The workforce is twelve departments, one skill each, covering every area of a company and of the Founder's personal life. Renamed: the `sales` skill is now `sales-and-marketing`, named for its department, and its team Sales & Revenue (S&R) is now Sales & Marketing (S&M), with its roster at `agents/Sales & Marketing (S&M).md`. Merged: the Product Marketing Manager is renamed the Marketing Manager (`agents/Marketing Manager.md`) and absorbs the Demand Generation Manager, so one seat now holds positioning, messaging and launches and the programs that create qualified demand (paid, search and content, email and lifecycle nurture) inside the CAC ceiling, with positioning always first; the team is five seats with the ★ Chief Revenue Officer as lead. Personal life joins the scope: the Founder's personal brand, job search, networking, and negotiating pay or a deal, each seat running its craft for the Founder too, with a new `references/personal.md` and two new templates (`templates/job-search.md`, `templates/negotiation.md`). Every reference, template, spec and the roster updated for the merged seat and the personal scope; the personal context and health rules added to the roster's global rules and the skill; every other department named by its new skill (administration, finance-and-accounting, customer-experience, public-relations, research-and-development, business-intelligence, legal-and-compliance, information-technology, human-resources, quality-assurance), with what the product can do now asked of the research-and-development skill's ★ Head of Research & Development and a regulated or comparative claim gated by the legal-and-compliance skill's Compliance Officer; the trigger test redrawn for the merged and personal scope.

### `1.0.0` - 2026-11-07

Initial build of the sales skill: the Sales & Revenue (S&R) department, which carries the marketing seats, becomes its own skill, laid out like discover, research and finance. The skill runs as the ★ Chief Revenue Officer and dispatches the Account Executive, the Revenue Operations Manager, the Sales Engineer, the Product Marketing Manager and the Demand Generation Manager, scaled by tier (quick, standard, deep), to one standard: one funnel, one honest number. The roster takes develop's format with self-contained global rules; each spec points its Identity at the roster, and its mentions of retired seats and fixed venture paths now name the skill that owns the work (the board skill's ★ Chief Executive Officer, the develop skill's ★ Engineering Lead, the customers skill's Client Experience team) or the records the repository's instructions keep. Four references (deliverables, dispatch, the forecast discipline, the deal desk), three templates (the brief, the forecast call, the go-to-market plan) and a trigger test. Consequential work is verified by the quality skill's ★ Head of Quality, and anything sent to a prospect or the public, any offer, spend or contract, waits for the user.
