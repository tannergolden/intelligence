# Modes router - the front door

*How to read a request, pick exactly one mode, run the preflight (on the venture, or on the Founder's own affairs), and convene the right workforce seats. This is the first reference every run reads (`SKILL.md`, phase 0). The router decides nothing on its own; it routes to a mode, and the mode does the work.*

A run is one mode on one venture, or on one personal matter. The five modes (found, strategize, decide, operate, review) share one team, one preflight, and one write-gate (`write-gate.md`). Routing is two questions answered in order: **is this administration's job at all** (or does it belong to another department's skill), and if so **which mode**. Answer the first with the near-miss negatives below, the second with the signal table. When the request is underspecified (no clear venture or life area, question, options, or criteria where they matter), ask 1 to 3 framing questions before routing, not after.

## Signal phrase to mode

The request's verb is the strongest signal. Map it, then confirm against the "fires on" column before announcing the mode.

| Signal phrase in the request | Mode | Why this mode |
|---|---|---|
| "found a company", "start a venture", "we have an idea for X", "incorporate", "stand up the company" | **found** | Stands up a new venture: scaffold its record (from the repository's venture template if its instructions name one, else this skill's `templates/`; `SKILL.md`, Where it runs), run the gated idea-to-launch checklist, write `Overview.md` / `Goals.md` / `Strategy.md` at the starting stage |
| "set our strategy", "refresh the OKRs", "what is our north star", "plan the quarter", "plan the year", "should we enter market X"; for the Founder, "help me set my goals for the year", "what should my priorities be", "plan the next five years of my life" | **strategize** | Runs a planning cycle: set or refresh the thesis and the OKRs, pick the strategy framework, write `Strategy.md` and the `Goals.md` the OKRs ladder up to; for the Founder, the life strategy and the goals for the year and the quarter (`strategy-toolkit.md`, Group F) |
| "should I", "should we", "which should I choose", "go or no-go", "build vs buy", "make the call on X", "weigh the options and recommend one"; for the Founder, "should I take this job", "should I move to X", "should I buy X" | **decide** | The multi-perspective decision engine: independent views from the departments, structured dissent, one accountable decider, a BLUF memo with the minority view, recorded to `Administration/Decisions.md` and `Log.md`, or for a life choice, to the Founder's own records where a private repository allows, with the Founder as the decider |
| "run the venture", "execute the plan", "launch the product across the company", "get us ready for the raise", "stand up the launch with sales, legal and support"; for the Founder, "get everything ready for my move" | **operate** | Runs work that crosses departments: the chair splits it into each department's part, convenes each department's lead or hands the part to its skill (`routing.md`), the quality-assurance skill's ★ Head of Quality verifies, and administration writes each result into the owning department's records behind the write-gate |
| "how is the venture doing", "run the weekly", "run the QBR", "grade the OKRs", "keep it current", "health check", recurring or scheduled upkeep; for the Founder, "review my week", "what should I focus on this week", "what did I commit to", "how am I doing on my goals" | **review** | Runs the operating cadence (weekly business review, monthly financials, quarterly OKR grading and QBR), health-checks the venture, keeps `Overview` / `Goals` / `Log` current, and advances or flags the lifecycle stage; for the Founder, the weekly review of goals, priorities, calendar and commitments, and the quarterly goals check (`operating-cadence.md`) |

Tie-break rule: when two rows fit, the verb wins over the noun. "Should we set a new strategy" is **decide** if it is asking for a yes-or-no on one move, **strategize** if it is asking to actually run the planning cycle and produce the plan; ask one framing question if the line is genuinely unclear. A request that asks to both decide and then produce ("decide whether to rebrand, and if yes draft it") is two runs, sequenced: decide first, then operate on the outcome. One mode and one venture, or one personal matter, per run; never collapse two modes into one pass.

## The preflight (runs before every mode)

No mode runs context-blind. Before framing anything, the chair (Chief Executive Officer) runs the same preflight on the named venture, or on the Founder's own affairs, so the call or the deliverable is grounded in the real context and not in the request alone.

1. **Resolve the subject.** Identify which venture the request targets and where its record lives: the venture folder or repository the repository's instructions name, or, with no such instruction, the conversation (`SKILL.md`, Where it runs). If none is named and the request is ambiguous, ask. If the mode is **found** and no venture exists yet, the preflight is deferred: found scaffolds the venture's record first (from the repository's venture template if its instructions name one, else this skill's `templates/`, giving each new file the frontmatter and naming the repository requires), then proceeds. On a personal matter, the subject is the Founder's own affairs: their records live only in a private repository whose instructions say it was made to hold them, where its root and folder `README.md` files say, or else in the conversation.
2. **Read the spine.** For a venture, read `Overview.md`, `Goals.md`, `Strategy.md` (if present), and the recent tail of `Log.md`, or, for a venture kept in the conversation, what the user has given. These carry the thesis, the live OKRs, and the running record. For the Founder, read their goals and priorities, their open commitments and calendar as they share it, and the decisions already settled. A decision that contradicts a settled one, or a deliverable that duplicates an existing one, is caught here.
3. **Detect the stage (a venture only).** Read the declared stage from `Overview.md`, then check it against the real signals (revenue, headcount, retention, last round) per `lifecycle.md`. The detected stage, not just the declared string, sets the convened roster, the rigor, the metrics, and the cadence for the run. A pre-revenue idea and a mature company are not run the same way. A personal matter has no stage; its stakes and its reversibility set the tier.
4. **Dedup check.** Search the record, and the repository when there is one, for the question or the deliverable so a settled decision is not re-litigated and existing context is not duplicated.
5. **Conformance read.** Because every mode may end in a write, read the repository's own instructions (its `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md`, or with neither its root and folder `README.md` files) and the rules of any folder to be written into, and commit an existing file's last state before editing it (git history is the backup). With no repository, there is nothing to read: the run delivers in chat. The write itself is the skill's, behind the write-gate; the preflight only makes the run ready to write.

Only after the preflight does the chair frame the exact question, deliverable, or plan and convene the departments the work touches. The preflight is identical across modes; what changes downstream is which seats are convened and whether the run deliberates (decide), produces (operate), plans (strategize, found), or grades (review).

## Routing to the right workforce seats

Routing does not stop at the mode; it names the opening seats so the chair convenes the smallest sufficient roster (`dispatch.md` scales the tier on every run; `specialist-dispatch.md` covers how administration reaches a department, and how a lead reaches its specialists, in operate). The chair (Chief Executive Officer) holds every run. Each department lead below sits in its own skill's `agents/`. Beyond the chair:

- **found** opens with the chair (Chief Executive Officer) framing the thesis and the riskiest assumption, the legal-and-compliance skill's ★ General Counsel for entity choice and the irreversible gates (83(b) within 30 days, QSBS 5-year clock), and the finance-and-accounting skill (its ★ Chief Financial Officer) for founder equity and runway. The research-and-development skill brings the customer evidence. The Corporate Secretary composes the governance record. See `founding-playbook.md`.
- **strategize** opens with the chair (Chief Executive Officer) picking the framework and framing the options, the finance-and-accounting skill for the financial envelope, and the department leads the thesis touches; the COO when ventures or projects compete for the same resources. For the Founder's own goals, the chair frames, the departments whose personal scope the goals touch are heard, and the Founder decides. See `strategy-toolkit.md` and `metrics-and-kpis.md`.
- **decide** convenes only the lenses the decision touches (`lenses.md`), each returning an independent written view in parallel, plus one dissent role (the quality-assurance skill's Devil's Advocate, or a pre-mortem), the Corporate Secretary for the record, on deep runs the COO for the execution plan, and the Chief of Staff to carry the call to every department it binds. The decider is one seat; on a product decision, the CEO; on a personal decision, the Founder. See `deliberation.md`, `frameworks.md`, `synthesis.md`, `memo.md`.
- **operate** splits the work into each department's part and routes each part to the skill that owns it (`routing.md`: the finance-and-accounting skill to `Financials.md`, the sales-and-marketing skill's ★ Chief Revenue Officer to `Brand.md`, the operations-and-logistics skill's ★ Head of Operations to `Operations.md`, the business-intelligence skill's ★ Chief Data Officer to `Metrics.md`, and so on). Each part's lead frames a brief and dispatches its specialists, or the department skill runs the part as a whole deliverable; the quality-assurance skill's ★ Head of Quality verifies before administration writes. See `specialist-dispatch.md`, `routing.md`, `verification.md`, `write-gate.md`.
- **review** convenes the COO for the operating cadence, the finance-and-accounting skill for financials-versus-plan, the business-intelligence skill for the numbers, the chair (Chief Executive Officer) grading the OKRs, and the department leads whose metrics moved. For the Founder's weekly review, the Chief of Staff brings the commitments and the calendar, and the chair the goals and priorities. See `operating-cadence.md`.

The standard holds in every mode: **every angle heard, one owner accountable.** Every relevant department is heard before the call converges, work is verified before it lands, and the run resolves to a single recommendation or deliverable with one accountable owner. Departments form their views in isolation and no department grades its own input; the chair alone holds the synthesis; and administration alone writes, behind the write-gate.

## Near-miss negatives (route elsewhere)

Administration runs the company and steers the Founder's own affairs; it is not the only skill near that work. Route the following away before picking a mode. The test is the verb and the deliverable, not the topic.

- **One department's own work routes to that department's skill.** Each department is its own skill and does its own work, called alone, for a company and for the Founder: "draft an NDA with our supplier" or "review my lease" is `legal-and-compliance`, "write the onboarding SOP" or "plan the logistics of my move" is `operations-and-logistics`, "draft the brand guide", "forecast this quarter's pipeline" or "help me negotiate my salary" is `sales-and-marketing`, "set up compensation bands" or "update my career profile" is `human-resources`, "review our cloud access controls" or "secure my accounts" is `information-technology`, "define how we calculate churn" or "track my sleep and workouts" is `business-intelligence`, "write the test strategy for the release" or "check my application before I submit it" is `quality-assurance`, "build the customer onboarding playbook" or "get a refund for this order" is `customer-experience`, "write the press release for the funding round" or "write the email declining the offer" is `public-relations`. Administration takes it only when it crosses departments, is a company decision, or is a life choice ("should we sign an exclusive deal with this supplier", "launch the product across sales, legal and support", "should I move to Denver"), and then convenes the department inside its run (`routing.md`).
- **Money routes to `finance-and-accounting`.** "Close the books for October", "build the financial model", "what is our runway" are its, whose ★ Chief Financial Officer is the company's finance function; so is the Founder's personal and household money ("can I afford this house", "should I refinance my mortgage", "pay off the car loan or invest"). Administration convenes it for the figures inside a run whose question is bigger than the money: a life choice weighed against more than its cost, or a company call.
- **Pure ideation, divergence, "what are my options" routes to `research-and-development`.** It *generates* options; administration *decides among them and acts on them*. "Brainstorm ways to grow the audience", "what are my options for monetizing this", "give me ten ideas for the launch" are its. The moment the request asks to choose among options or to act on one ("which should I choose", "should we do X", "run the venture"), it is administration's. Found is the boundary case: "we have an idea for X, let's start the company" is administration (found) because it asks to *stand up and act*, not to *generate ideas*; pure idea generation with no intent to found stays in research-and-development.
- **Fact-finding, "what is known" routes to `research-and-development`.** It *finds and verifies* what is known; administration *uses it to run the company or to decide*. "Research the market size for this product", "what is the current state of X", "get me sources on the competitor" are its. Administration convenes it (and the business-intelligence skill for the company's own numbers) as an *input* to a mode, but a request whose whole job is the evidence, not a call or a deliverable, is research-and-development's.
- **Building a skill, an agent, a team, a tool or the product routes to `research-and-development`.** It builds the workforce, the company's product, and the Founder's tools and side projects (its ★ Head of Research & Development holds technical direction); administration runs ventures with what it built and decides what the product should be. "Build me a skill that makes decisions", "create a new agent for the sales team", "turn this workflow into a team", "build the MVP we decided on" are its. Administration never edits a roster, its own in `agents/` or a department skill's, and never authors agents; it convenes them, and a roster change is research-and-development's.
- **Writing or polishing a single piece of prose routes to `public-relations`.** Its Writer and Editor write and refine one piece; administration orchestrates the whole and may *dispatch* it as a producer inside an operate run. "Write up the decision once it is made", "tighten this memo", "proofread the press release" are its. The signal is a single self-contained piece of writing with no cross-department deliberation or production behind it.
- **A chart, diagram, or visual routes to `business-intelligence`.** Its Visualization Designer renders one graphic; administration may dispatch it as a producer within operate, but "make a chart of the revenue", "diagram the org", "render this as a dashboard image" with no call to make is its, not an administration run.
- **Checking a piece of work routes to `quality-assurance`.** "Check this before it goes out", "verify this against the acceptance criteria", "fact-check this report" are its; administration calls it inside a run to verify, and never grades its own synthesis.

When a near-miss negative fires, say which skill owns it and stop; do not start an administration run to be helpful. When the request genuinely spans administration and another department (decide a call, then have public-relations draft the announcement), administration runs its mode and *dispatches* the other as a producer behind the write-gate, rather than handing the whole request away.

## Routing summary

1. Run the near-miss test first: one department's own work to that department's skill, money to finance-and-accounting, ideation, fact-finding and building to research-and-development, a single piece of writing to public-relations, a chart to business-intelligence, a check to quality-assurance. If any fires, route away and stop.
2. If it is administration's job, map the request's verb to one mode with the signal table; break ties by verb over noun and by asking one framing question when the line is unclear.
3. Run the preflight on the named venture (resolve, read the spine, detect the stage, dedup, conformance read), or on the Founder's own affairs (resolve where their records may live, read their goals and commitments, dedup, conformance read); found defers it and scaffolds first.
4. Announce the mode and the tier (a cost decision made with the owner's tokens), convene the smallest sufficient roster, and hand off to the mode's references.

One mode, one venture or one personal matter, one run. Every angle heard, one owner accountable.
