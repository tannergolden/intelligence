# Routing - which skill owns each deliverable

*The map administration routes work by: each deliverable family, the department skill that owns it, its lead, what it does for a company and for the Founder's own life, and what it needs from other departments. Administration's own deliverables, which it produces itself, are kept here in full. Each department's detailed catalog (every deliverable's producing seat, trigger, Definition of Done, and file) lives in its own skill's references (its `references/deliverables.md` where it keeps one), which win wherever this map differs.*

Read this with `lenses.md` (what each department brings on a decide run), `specialist-dispatch.md` (how administration convenes a department and hands it work), and `deliverable-dependency-chains.md` (the producing order). The same lead that advises on a decide run owns the deliverable on an operate run. Every artifact resolves to one owning skill, one accountable lead, and one owning file: **every angle heard, one owner accountable**, in producing form. No agent writes; within an administration run, agents and the department skills compose and return, and administration persists.

## One department, or across departments

- **One department's work, asked for on its own, is that skill's run.** "Draft an NDA with our supplier" is the legal-and-compliance skill's; "close the books for October" is the finance-and-accounting skill's; "plan my trip to Lisbon" is the operations-and-logistics skill's; "write the email declining the offer" is the public-relations skill's. Route it there and stop: the department skill does the work and persists its own records.
- **Administration runs it when it crosses departments, is a company decision, or is a life choice.** A launch, a raise, a reorganization, a plan that binds several departments, a call the company has to make, or a personal decision that weighs several departments' views (a move, a job offer, a big purchase): administration frames it, convenes each department's part per the map below (by dispatching that department's lead from its skill's `agents/`, or by handing the skill the whole deliverable), the quality-assurance skill's ★ Head of Quality verifies, and administration persists the results.

Stage scales what is produced. A **found**-stage venture produces the lean, runway-protecting subset (a one-page model, a lean canvas, a founder-led GTM note); a **scale** venture produces the repeatable-motion set (the 3-statement model, the sales playbook, the org design); a **maintain** venture produces the durability set (the board package, the QBR, the anti-aging product review). Convene the smallest sufficient set of departments for the stage, or for the stakes of a personal call, never the full workforce by reflex (`lifecycle.md`, `dispatch.md`).

## Administration (its own) - ★ Chief Executive Officer

The executive office: enterprise direction, the product decision, cross-department allocation, the portfolio across ventures and projects, strategy framing, the governance record, and the follow-through; and for the Founder, their life strategy, goals and priorities, the big decisions of their life, and their calendar and commitments. Owns the venture's strategic core and its decision history. On a decide run this office runs the deliberation; on an operate run it produces the strategy, the product decisions and the cadence artifacts that the departments' work, the research-and-development skill's roadmap among it, ladders up to.

- **Standing assets:** the company thesis and strategy; the product decisions (the roadmap they set is the research-and-development skill's); the decision and governance log; the operating-cadence record and the commitment log; for the Founder, their goals and priorities, their personal decision record, and their commitments.
- **Recurring outputs:**
  - **Strategy / business plan** (Chief Executive Officer, as chair): the thesis, the market and competitor surface, the chosen framework's output, the OKRs the departments ladder to. Trigger: a strategize run or a stage change. Lands in **Administration/Strategy.md**.
  - **Product decisions** (Chief Executive Officer): what to build, for whom, and when, and the release go or no-go. Each rests on the research-and-development skill's evidence about users and markets, weighs its options, and is built by it, its ★ Head of Research & Development holding technical direction and feasibility. Trigger: a planning cycle, a launch, a pivot decision. The call lands in **Administration/Decisions.md**; the product roadmap it sets (the bets by horizon, the requirements a build is held to, the release plan) is the research-and-development skill's, kept by its ★ Head of Research & Development in its own records (**Research & Development (R&D)/Roadmap.md** where a venture keeps department folders).
  - **Decision record** (Corporate Secretary composes, administration writes): the BLUF call, the rationale, the convened departments, the minority view by role, the owner, the success measure. Trigger: every decide run. Lands in **Administration/Decisions.md** (`memo.md`).
  - **Operating cadence and board record** (Chief Operating Officer): the weekly / monthly / quarterly rhythm, the board and QBR pre-reads, tracked follow-through. Trigger: a review run. Lands in **Administration/Cadence.md** (`operating-cadence.md`).
  - **Execution plan** (Chief Operating Officer): the decided call turned into owned, dated next steps across departments. Trigger: a deep decide run or an operate kickoff. Folded into **Administration/Cadence.md** and the venture `Log.md`.
  - **Portfolio allocation** (Chief Operating Officer recommends; the CEO or the Founder allocates): the RICE-scored, balanced mix across ventures and projects competing for the same capital, time or attention, the Founder's personal projects included. Trigger: a planning cycle with more than one venture or project, or a new contender for the same resources. Lands in **Administration/Strategy.md**, the call in **Administration/Decisions.md**.
  - **Commitment log** (Chief of Staff): every commitment a decision created, with its owning department, its date, and its done-test, carried to each department it binds. Trigger: every decision that binds a department. Folded into **Administration/Cadence.md**.
- **For the Founder's own life** (each is personal context, written only where a private repository made to hold it says, by its instructions, and otherwise returned in the conversation):
  - **Life strategy, goals and priorities** (Chief Executive Officer frames; the Founder decides): the few goals for the year, the quarter's key results, and the week's priorities, laddered to what the Founder says matters. Trigger: a strategize run, the turn of a year or a quarter, a life event.
  - **Personal decision record** (Corporate Secretary composes): the life choice, the options weighed, each convened department's view, the minority view, and the Founder as the decider. Trigger: every personal decide run.
  - **Weekly review and commitments** (Chief of Staff): the week's priorities, the calendar's conflicts, and every open commitment with its date. Trigger: a review run, weekly.
- **DoD, Strategy.md:** thesis stated in one paragraph; a named strategy framework with its output; OKRs that are specific and measurable and ladder to the thesis; the metric definitions referenced to **Metrics.md**, not redefined here.
- **DoD, a product decision (in Decisions.md):** it ties to a strategy objective in **Strategy.md** or **Goals.md**; it cites the evidence it rests on, and an invalidated assumption is recorded as such, not quietly dropped; the scope it hands the research-and-development skill for its roadmap is stated so that each requirement can carry acceptance criteria a verifier can check.
- **DoD, Decisions.md:** one decision per entry with the call, the rationale, the convened departments, the minority view, the single owner, the success measure, and the reversibility class; nothing material left to memory.
- **DoD, Cadence.md:** every action and commitment has one owner, a date, and a done-test; the cadence record shows which loop ran, what it graded, and what it reset.
- **DoD, the Founder's goals and decisions:** each goal ladders to something the Founder said matters and has a measure and a date; each personal decision names the Founder as the decider and the options not taken; nothing lands outside a private repository made to hold it.

Each department's company files sit in its own folder of the venture's record, named by default for its team (`Legal & Compliance (L&C)/Compliance.md`, `Finance & Accounting (F&A)/Financial-Model.md`), unless the repository's instructions or its venture template name another. The Founder's own records go only where a private repository made to hold them says, by its instructions; anywhere else they stay in the conversation. The finance-and-accounting skill's ★ Chief Financial Officer is the company's finance function and routes the work inside its team. The quality-assurance skill's ★ Head of Quality is also the independent verifier of every other department's consequential deliverable in an administration run (`verification.md`).

## Cross-department dependencies

A department does not build on a missing input from another; administration sequences the parts (`deliverable-dependency-chains.md`).

- **finance-and-accounting** needs the strategy and OKRs its model ladders to (administration), the pipeline and revenue plan (sales-and-marketing), the hiring plan and comp bands (human-resources), the metric definitions (business-intelligence), and the entity facts behind the cap table, the 83(b) and QSBS (legal-and-compliance). Cap-table changes and moving money are Founder-gated.
- **legal-and-compliance** needs the deal terms (sales-and-marketing), the vendor terms (operations-and-logistics), the offer terms (human-resources), the data flows and sub-processors (information-technology), and the cap-table effect of a formation or a round (finance-and-accounting). Signing and sending are Founder-gated.
- **sales-and-marketing** needs the strategy (administration), the evidence about the market (research-and-development), the metric definitions (business-intelligence), the contract terms it sells on (legal-and-compliance), and the brand-voice consistency gate on anything published (public-relations); it hands each closed customer to customer-experience.
- **operations-and-logistics** needs each department's process owner, the vendor terms (legal-and-compliance), and the spend line (finance-and-accounting). Committing spend or signing a supplier is Founder-gated.
- **human-resources** needs the strategy and the stage (administration), the runway a plan is costed against (finance-and-accounting), and the employment and offer terms (legal-and-compliance). An actual offer is Founder-gated.
- **information-technology** needs the product's build (research-and-development), the privacy obligations (legal-and-compliance), and the tooling vendors (operations-and-logistics). Its CISO, on the IT team, holds the security-risk line straight to the Founder (`workforce.md`).
- **research-and-development** needs the strategy and the product decisions its roadmap and builds follow (administration), and the security and platform constraints (information-technology); the quality-assurance skill checks its research and tests its releases.
- **business-intelligence** needs the North Star chosen in strategize (administration), each department's KPI owner, and the source systems (information-technology). Every other department's KPIs reference its definitions rather than redefine them.
- **quality-assurance** needs the deliverable, its brief, its Definition of Done, and its completion evidence from the producing skill.
- **customer-experience** needs the closed deal and its terms (sales-and-marketing), the product (research-and-development), and the metric definitions (business-intelligence); its ticket themes feed the CEO's product decisions.
- **public-relations** needs positioning and brand (sales-and-marketing), a legal read of every external claim (legal-and-compliance), and the decision being announced (administration). Any external publication is Founder-gated.
- **Product**: the research-and-development skill's evidence comes before the decision, its options are weighed in it, its roadmap carries the decision's scope into requirements, it builds only what the decision defined, and the quality-assurance skill tests the release.
- **A personal decision**: the Founder's goals and values come first (from what they say, or from the human-resources skill's profile where a private repository holds it), then each convened department's view (finance-and-accounting on the money, legal-and-compliance on the lease or contract, operations-and-logistics on the logistics, human-resources on career and wellbeing, sales-and-marketing on negotiating the pay or the deal), then the Founder's call; the acts that carry it out (accepting, signing, paying, sending) are the Founder's.

## The write rule (read with write-gate.md)

For each deliverable in an administration run: the chair frames it, the owning department's lead frames the brief and its specialists produce (or the department skill runs its own loop on administration's brief and returns the result), the quality-assurance skill's ★ Head of Quality verifies it against its Definition of Done, and **administration writes** the verified artifact into the owning file, with the existing file's last state committed first (in a repository) and a one-line entry appended to the venture's `Log.md`. Internal drafts are autonomous; anything irreversible or external (moving money, signing or sending a contract, publishing or sending any message outside the company or the household, deleting a file, changing equity or the cap table) is hard-gated and escalates to the Founder before the write. The owning file is the single home for the artifact: every angle heard in producing it, one lead accountable for it, one writer who lands it.
