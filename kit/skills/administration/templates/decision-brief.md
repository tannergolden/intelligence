# Decision brief (the chair fills this in Phase 0)

A self-contained brief that frames the decision before any view is taken, threaded into every department-lead dispatch so each parallel worker reasons against the real question rather than improvising it. It frames a company call and a life choice alike.

- **The decision:** [the raw decision or question, as the Founder gave it]
- **The question (SCQA):** [Situation: the shared context. Complication: what changed, why now. Question: the exact choice on the table, at the right altitude, with no baked-in answer.]
- **Options:** [the 2 to 3 genuine, MECE options the chair (the CEO) laid out]
- **Decision criteria:** [what a good call optimizes for; how the options will be judged. On a personal call, the Founder's own goals and values, in their words]
- **Lenses to convene:** [only the departments the decision touches, each lead named for its skill (the legal-and-compliance skill's ★ General Counsel); on a personal call, the departments whose personal scope it touches; see references/lenses.md]
- **Dissent:** [the quality-assurance skill's Devil's Advocate, a pre-mortem, or both]
- **Framework(s):** [the dissent or scoring tool(s) this run will use; see references/frameworks.md]
- **Decider and escalation:** [CEO for reversible in-mandate company calls; Founder for irreversible or major ones, and for every personal call]
- **Constraints and context:** [hard limits, what is fixed versus open, any evidence or data that grounds it]
- **Where the record goes:** [the venture's Administration/Decisions.md; or, for a personal call, the Founder's records only where a private repository made to hold them says, else the conversation]
