# Decision memo (administration, decide mode)

A board-style decision record. The skill fills this and appends it to the venture's `Administration/Decisions.md` (or, for a cross-venture call with no venture record, files it where the repository's instructions keep reports or at the path the user names, and otherwise returns it in chat), then adds a one-line entry to the venture's `Log.md`. A memo on the Founder's own life (a move, a job offer, a big purchase) is personal context: it is filed only where a private repository made to hold it says, and otherwise returned in chat, with the Founder as the decider. Lead with the answer; preserve the minority view. The convened departments and their leads are derived from the brief, not fixed.
