# Dispatch - how the tiers map onto the workforce

How `board` spends its workforce (`workforce.md`), scaled to the decision. Read before choosing a tier. The workforce is the chair (Chief Executive Officer) plus the Administration tier (Corporate Secretary, Chief Operating Officer) and the 13 functional department leads (BI, CX, F&A, HR, Information Security, IT, L&C, O&L, Product Technology, PR, QA, R&D, S&R). Every dispatch is a self-contained brief; the subagent sees none of the conversation; functional leads run in parallel and never see each other's views while forming their own; the chair alone holds the question, synthesizes, and names the owner.

## Tier decision table

| If the decision is | Tier | Convene |
|---|---|---|
| small, low-stakes, or inside one function | **quick** | chair plus the 1 to 2 relevant leads; no formal dissent pass; call it |
| a real cross-functional call worth the board | **standard** | chair (who lays out the MECE options in framing) plus the relevant functional leads (independent views), one dissent role (devil's advocate or pre-mortem), and the Corporate Secretary (record) |
| high-stakes, irreversible, contested, or cross-venture | **deep / board-grade** | the full relevant board, a dissent battery (devil's advocacy, pre-mortem, red team), a scoring or dialectical pass, a scenario test, and the COO (execution plan); escalate to the Founder as decider |

When two rows fit, take the higher. Announce the tier before dispatching; it is a cost decision made with the owner's tokens. One decision per run.

## How each seat is briefed

- **The chair (CEO).** Frames the question (SCQA), lays out a MECE set of 2 to 3 genuine options and their second-order effects in framing, flags each as reversible or irreversible and recommends (the Founder decides major calls), names the relevant lenses and the criteria, runs the synthesis, and states a view last. Not dispatched as a worker; the chair is the run.
- **Functional leads (the lenses).** Each briefed with the same framed question, the same criteria, and the output contract: an independent written view (recommendation, assumptions, tradeoffs). Dispatched in parallel, blind to each other. Convene only the lenses the decision touches (`lenses.md`).
- **Dissent role.** Briefed to argue against the front-runner (devil's advocate) or to imagine the decision has failed and work back (pre-mortem). On deep, a red team and a dialectical counter-plan are separate briefs.
- **Corporate Secretary.** Briefed to record the decision memo (`memo.md`), including the minority view by role.
- **Chief Operating Officer.** The honest broker; briefed on deep runs to pressure-test the synthesis for the chair before the call hardens, and to turn the decided call into an execution plan (the next steps, the owners, the dates).

## The one rule that does not bend

The chair holds the question and the synthesis. Functional leads form views in isolation and advise; no lead grades its own view, and no lead synthesizes. The decider is one seat (the CEO, or the Founder on major calls), and the decision names one accountable owner. This is the standard in operating form: every angle heard, one owner accountable.
