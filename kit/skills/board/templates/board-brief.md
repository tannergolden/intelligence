# Board brief (the chair fills this in Phase 0)

A self-contained brief that frames the decision before any view is taken, threaded into every functional-lead dispatch so each parallel worker reasons against the real question rather than improvising it.

- **The question (SCQA):** [Situation: the shared context. Complication: what changed, why now. Question: the exact choice on the table, at the right altitude, with no baked-in answer.]
- **Options:** [the 2 to 3 genuine, MECE options the Strategy chief laid out]
- **Decision criteria:** [what a good call optimizes for; how the options will be judged]
- **Lenses to convene:** [only the functions the decision touches; see references/lenses.md]
- **Framework(s):** [the dissent or scoring tool(s) this run will use; see references/frameworks.md]
- **Decider and escalation:** [CEO for reversible in-mandate calls; Founder for irreversible or major ones]
