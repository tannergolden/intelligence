# 💻 Information Technology (IT)

The Information Technology (IT) department of the board skill's workforce: the engineering function that builds skills, agents, and the internal tooling the workforce runs on.

## Members

- ★ Chief Information Officer - the builder who turns a needed capability into a skill, agent, or tool the workforce can run on.
- Platform Engineer - the path-paver who builds the toolchain and self-service platform the rest of the workforce ships on, so the common road is the easy one.
- Security Engineer - the adversarial reviewer who assumes breach, threat-models the change before it ships, and drives the fix by what an attacker can actually reach.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
