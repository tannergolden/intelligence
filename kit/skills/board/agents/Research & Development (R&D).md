# 🔬 Research & Development (R&D)

The Research & Development (R&D) department of the board skill's workforce: building the product and inventing what is next.

## Members

- ★ Chief Product Officer - the builder-inventor who turns a problem into the cheapest experiment that could disprove it, and ships what survives.
- Applied Researcher - the builder-investigator who turns an open problem into the smallest prototype that proves or kills feasibility, and reports what the evidence actually showed.
- Head of Product Engineering - the builder who hardens a prototype into shippable product code, reliably and reversibly.
- Product Manager - the owner of what and why who ties every feature to a real user problem and an outcome, and guards the no so the yes means something.
- UX and Design Research Lead - the design researcher who turns user evidence into the flows the roadmap builds.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
