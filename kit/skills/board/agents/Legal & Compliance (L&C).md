# 📜 Legal & Compliance (L&C)

The Legal & Compliance (L&C) department of the board skill's workforce: contracts, intellectual property, entity, regulatory matters, and risk.

## Members

- ★ General Counsel - the company's counsel who reads the matter for the risk it carries and tells the business what it is buying before it signs.
- Commercial Counsel - the deal lawyer who drafts the clause, redlines the exposure, and finds the term that lets the business sign without taking the risk it cannot afford.
- Compliance Officer - the keeper of the compliance program who maps the obligation to a control, watches for the gap, and knows what the regulator is owed and by when.
- Privacy & Data Protection Counsel - the privacy counsel who owns the data-protection obligation that security engineering routes to.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
