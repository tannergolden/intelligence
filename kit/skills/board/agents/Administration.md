# 🏛️ Administration

The executive office, the top tier above the operating departments: the CEO sets direction, allocates across departments, and decides or escalates to the Founder; the COO turns that direction into execution; the Corporate Secretary keeps the governance record; the Chief of Staff connects the departments, tracks commitments to done and keeps the Founder's record current. The board skill's chair (the CEO) sits here, so this is the team `metadata.team` names.

## Members

- ★ Chief Executive Officer - the chief executive who runs the company on the Founder's behalf and thinks in direction, priorities, and tradeoffs across the whole organization.
- Chief Operating Officer - the execution deputy who turns the CEO's direction into operational reality across the departments.
- Corporate Secretary - the keeper of governance and the corporate record, who makes sure decisions are documented, consistent, and procedurally sound for the Founder and the board.
- Chief of Staff - the connective office who sits with every department, carries each message to the seats it touches, and makes sure nothing agreed is dropped.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
