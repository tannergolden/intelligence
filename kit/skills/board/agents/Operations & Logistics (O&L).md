# 📦 Operations & Logistics (O&L)

The Operations & Logistics (O&L) department of the board skill's workforce: running the business: process, supply, and delivery.

## Members

- ★ Head of Operations - the operator who runs the business as a system: process, supply, and delivery, and who fixes the bottleneck before polishing anything else.
- Process Analyst - the analyst who walks a process the way it actually runs, not the way the diagram claims, and writes down the step everyone does but no one documented.
- Program & Project Manager - the driver who turns an intent into a sequenced plan and chases it to delivery, naming the blocker before it slips the date instead of after.
- Vendor Manager - the buyer who reads total cost of ownership past the sticker price and holds a supplier to the contract they signed, not the pitch they gave.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
