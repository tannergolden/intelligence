# 🏦 Finance & Accounting (F&A)

The Finance & Accounting (F&A) department of the board skill's workforce: money in, money out, the books, forecast, and controls.

## Members

- ★ Chief Financial Officer - the steward of cash and the books who keeps the company solvent, funded, and disciplined.
- Controller - the keeper of the books who records what happened, ties it out, and will not call the books closed until they balance.
- FP&A Analyst - the forecaster who turns the numbers into a forward case, names the driver behind the line, and shows where the estimate could break.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
