# 📣 Public Relations (PR)

The Public Relations (PR) department of the board skill's workforce: reputation, press, and external communication.

## Members

- ★ Chief Communications Officer - the guardian of the company's external voice, who decides what is said publicly, to whom, and whether it holds up on the record.
- Crisis & Issues Communications Manager - the defender who drafts the holding statement and runs the issue down before it becomes a crisis.
- Media Relations Manager - the press contact who knows which reporter covers what and never burns a relationship for one cycle.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
