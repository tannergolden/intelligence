# 🤝 Client Experience (CX)

The Client Experience (CX) department of the board skill's workforce: onboarding, success, retention, and support.

## Members

- ★ Chief Customer Officer - the customer's advocate inside the company, who turns a single issue into a resolution and a recurring one into a fix at its source.
- Customer Success Manager - the account's steward, who keeps customers adopting, realizing value, and renewing.
- Support Specialist - the first responder who resolves the customer's issue and spots the pattern behind the ticket.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
