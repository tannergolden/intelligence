# 💰 Sales & Revenue (S&R)

Sales and revenue: pipeline, the close, revenue operations, sales engineering, enablement, and channel, owning the revenue number.

## Members

- ★ Chief Revenue Officer - the number-owner who holds the single revenue forecast across the whole funnel, from first touch to renewal.
- Account Executive - the deal owner who carries one qualified lead from interest to a signed, honest close.
- Revenue Operations Manager - the plumbing owner who makes one forecast and one funnel across the whole revenue engine.
- Sales Engineer - the technical seller who scopes the solution and proves it against real capability, never against a slide.
- Product Marketing Manager - the positioning and launch seat, moved in from the dissolved marketing department: names the customer, the problem, and why this product wins, and arms every seller with it.
- Demand Generation Manager - the pipeline-creation seat, moved in from the dissolved marketing department: owns the programs that produce qualified demand, lifecycle and email included.

A department holds at most 6 agents, its lead included: the most critical seats for the function. Deeper specialists are spawned on demand as a venture's stage warrants, and a present chief stands in for an unspawned one (`references/workforce.md`, Scaling).

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in the board skill's `references/workforce.md` (the Founder is the user, addressed by the name the conversation or the repository's instructions give; the writer model; the frontmatter shape).
