# Cadence - the money checkups a review run performs

What a review run checks and when. Read before a review run. Each checkup ends in the `templates/checkup.md` scorecard and leaves the next dated deadlines in `Memory/Dates.md`. Every date below is the usual US one: verify it each year at the source (`assets/official-sources.json`), since dates move for weekends and holidays and the rules change.

## Monthly: the cash flow review (standard tier)

- **Lucille:** actual spending against the plan; the true surplus; the irregular costs due next month and the sinking funds behind them; the emergency fund against its target.
- **Owen:** progress on the payoff plan; any promotional rate ending within ninety days; statements scanned for errors or fraud.
- **The skill:** the next month's dated items into `Memory/Dates.md`.

## Quarterly (standard tier)

- **Harriet:** estimated tax payments when income is not fully withheld (self-employment, investment income, a large bonus or sale).
- **Graham:** allocation drift against the rebalancing rule; rebalance only outside the bands.
- **Lucille:** a rounded net worth snapshot, offered to `Profile/Finances.md`; a subscription and recurring-cost audit twice a year.

## The annual calendar (deep tier for the annual review)

| When | Checkup | Seats |
|------|---------|-------|
| Mid-January | The prior year's fourth-quarter estimated payment | Harriet |
| Late January to mid-February | Tax forms arrive (W-2s, 1099s, brokerage statements); gather the documents | Harriet |
| February to mid-April | File, or extend; contributions to IRAs and HSAs for the prior year are due by the filing deadline, not the extension | Harriet, Graham, Elliot |
| Mid-April | The filing deadline and the first-quarter estimated payment | Harriet |
| Mid-June | The second-quarter estimated payment | Harriet |
| Mid-September | The third-quarter estimated payment | Harriet |
| Mid-October | The extended filing deadline | Harriet |
| Autumn | Workplace open enrollment: the health plan, HSA or FSA amounts, group coverage | Elliot, Clara |
| Mid-October to early December | Medicare open enrollment, when the Founder or a parent is on Medicare | Clara |
| November to mid-January | Marketplace open enrollment on HealthCare.gov, when not covered at work: enroll by mid-December for coverage from January 1; a state's own exchange sets its own dates | Clara |
| November and December | Year-end moves: harvest losses or gains, Roth conversions, required distributions, charitable giving, the last workplace plan deferrals, FSA balances, withholding checked against the safe harbor | Harriet, Graham, Elliot |
| Once a year, any month | Credit reports from all three bureaus (free at the official source), insurance renewals and coverage review, every beneficiary designation, the emergency fund re-sized, the full plan refreshed | Owen, Clara, Lucille, the lead |
| Every three to five years, and at every life event | The will, the powers of attorney and the health care directive reviewed | Clara |

## How a review run works

1. Pick the checkup from the calendar and the Founder's request; the annual review runs every row of the scorecard.
2. Preflight as for any run, then dispatch the seats the checkup names, each briefed on the period under review.
3. Fill the `templates/checkup.md` scorecard: each area on track, watch, or act, with its rounded evidence and its one next action and date.
4. Gate through the Plan Reviewer as for a standard run, deliver the scorecard findings-first, and write the next deadlines into `Memory/Dates.md`.
5. Offer the Profile updates the checkup surfaced (a new rounded net worth, a changed risk posture, a new policy), line by line.
