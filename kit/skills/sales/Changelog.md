## How This File Works

- **Tracks** changes to how the skill works: tiers, the team, references, templates, the output contract. Not below-PATCH tweaks.
- **Bump** PATCH for a fix, MINOR for a backward-compatible addition, MAJOR for a breaking change. Update the Current Version line and `metadata.version` with every entry.
- **4-entry cap** per section; a fifth drops the oldest, which the repository's history keeps.

### `1.0.0` - 2026-11-07

Initial build of the sales skill: the Sales & Revenue (S&R) department, which carries the marketing seats, becomes its own skill, laid out like discover, research and finance. The skill runs as the ★ Chief Revenue Officer and dispatches the Account Executive, the Revenue Operations Manager, the Sales Engineer, the Product Marketing Manager and the Demand Generation Manager, scaled by tier (quick, standard, deep), to one standard: one funnel, one honest number. The roster takes develop's format with self-contained global rules; each spec points its Identity at the roster, and its mentions of retired seats and fixed venture paths now name the skill that owns the work (the board skill's ★ Chief Executive Officer, the develop skill's ★ Engineering Lead, the customers skill's Client Experience team) or the records the repository's instructions keep. Four references (deliverables, dispatch, the forecast discipline, the deal desk), three templates (the brief, the forecast call, the go-to-market plan) and a trigger test. Consequential work is verified by the quality skill's ★ Head of Quality, and anything sent to a prospect or the public, any offer, spend or contract, waits for the user.
